⚠️ Immediate Cyber Incident Response Checklist

If you are experiencing an active breach, ransomware outbreak, or suspicious intrusion:

1. Isolate Systems Disconnect Ethernet cables or disable Wi-Fi immediately. Do NOT power off systems as volatile RAM contains critical forensic artifacts.
2. Preserve Evidence Export firewall, DNS, and server log files. Take screenshots of ransom splash screens, phishing headers, or unexpected prompts.
3. Submit Report Submit an incident via CIRS with your evidence files. Our investigative team will review and assign your case.
